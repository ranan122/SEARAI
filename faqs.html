FAQs

What teams ask first

Do you need access to our model?

No. We work from the product itself or from transcripts. We also ask for your target age range and a short description of how the product is meant to be used.

Does this replace our content safety review?

No. Content safety review checks for harmful content, and you still need it. A developmental review asks a different question: does the product respond the way a child of that age needs?

Can we train our model on the evaluation set?

No. The set is licensed for internal testing only. A test that a model has already studied stops telling you anything, much like an exam a student has seen in advance.

Who owns the evaluation set?

SEAR AI keeps ownership of the scenarios and the rubric. Your team receives a license to use them for internal testing.

Is this a certification or a legal audit?

No. We do not certify products, and we do not give legal advice. We give your team an expert developmental review and a way to measure progress.

How is pricing set?

The review is a fixed fee. We set the scope and the price after a first call, once we understand your product and the ages you serve.

Let's talk about what you are building

A 30-minute call is enough to tell you where your product stands. There is no charge and no obligation.
