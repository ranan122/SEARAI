About

Ran An, PhD

Founder of SEAR AI. Developmental psychologist.

RoleFounder, SEAR AI
FieldDevelopmental psychology
DoctorateUniversity of Pittsburgh
FocusHow children interact with AI

I am a developmental psychologist who studies how children interact with AI. Over years of longitudinal research, I followed hundreds of international families from the prenatal period through childhood, tracking how children grow and how parents and children interact.

I now translate that research into design and evaluation standards for child-facing AI: how a system should understand a child, how it should engage, and how it should protect and guide. I have also evaluated large language models as tools for reading parent-child interaction. My work appears at venues in developmental science, human-agent interaction, and AI.

I know what children need from a responsive partner at each age, and I know how AI systems are built and tested. Few people sit on both sides of that line.

How we work

Four commitments

AI does not replace caregivers

We do not help products stand in for parents or teachers. When a moment is more than an AI should handle, the right design brings a trusted adult in.

Evaluation only

Our scenarios are for testing. They are never licensed for training or fine-tuning, so a good score keeps its meaning.

Honest findings

The report says what we find. That includes what your product already does well, since those strengths are worth protecting.

Your details stay private

Scenarios may be reused in general form in later work. Client names, products, and findings are not.

Let's talk about what you are building

A 30-minute call is enough to tell you where your product stands. There is no charge and no obligation.
